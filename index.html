Our Heritage

A Home Built on Hospitality

Rooted in the mist-kissed High Ranges of Kerala, Mathews Corner carries forward a cherished family tradition of opening our doors with warmth, humility, and authentic care.

Generations of our family have lived among the tea slopes and cardamom plantations of Peermade and Paruthumpara. Here, welcoming someone across the threshold is never a transaction—it is an art of hospitality passed down around the kitchen stove, over steaming cups of estate-grown orthodox tea, and in long, unhurried conversations on the veranda.

We created Mathews Corner not as a crowded commercial resort or a bustling hotel, but as an intimate private haven. It is a home where travelers, families, and remote thinkers can step away from noise, breathe the clean high-altitude mountain air, and experience Kerala as it was meant to be felt: personal, peaceful, and sincere.
